# Image assets

The initial images were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, then encoded as optimized WebP files with Sharp. They are illustrative printing product photography. They do not document completed client jobs or a real company facility. Replace them with client-supplied photography when available. All consumed assets are saved inside this project.

## Final generation prompts

The eight service photographs below used the following exact shared prompt, with each scene inserted in place of `{scene}`:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: digital printing website product photograph, landscape 4:3. {scene} Photorealistic high-end editorial still life, subtle sunlight from upper left, soft shadows, beautiful minimalist styling, exact realistic physical products, close composition filling most frame. No people, no watermark, no web interface, no invented famous brand, no lettering outside products. Tasteful coherent cream, deep green and muted terracotta palette.
